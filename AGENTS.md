## Not finished

### More question types — planned, not built yet

LiveRate has 9 question types: Quiz (`quiz`), Score items (`rating`), Pick one (`poll`), Pick several (`multi`), Put in order (`rank`), Number (`number`), Open answer (`text`), Audience Q&A (`qa`) and Word cloud (`words`). The builder also has a one-tap **Yes / No / Not sure** shortcut (`lib/presets.ts`), which makes a Pick one question with those options. These are still to build:

1. **Split 100 points.** Voters spread 100 points across the options to show priority. This needs a new kind (`points`), a table with one row per voter and option holding the points, a check that the points add up to exactly 100, and a voter screen with +/- steppers and a running total. Results show the average points per option. Build this next: it's dot voting, the usual way a retro picks what to fix, and LiveRate now targets small meetups and team standups/retros. Once it exists, add it to the Sprint retro template in `lib/templates.ts`.

Follow-ups for the types already built:

- **Quiz (`quiz`).** Built. A Pick one question with a right answer (`focus_topics.quiz_correct`, the option's index) and a timer (`quiz_seconds`, 5–120, default 20). The owner starts each question from the results screen (`POST /api/admin/sessions/[code]/quiz`: start / end / stop); the running question lives on `sessions.quiz_focus_id` + `quiz_started_at`, and phones learn about it from the status poll (1.5 s while the presentation has a quiz; the CDN caches status for 1 s). Answers lock in once and are scored on arrival: right = 500 + up to 500 for speed, wrong = 0, accepted until 1.5 s after time is up. The right answer is never in the voters' copy of the presentation; it appears in status 2 s after time is up. Players pick a nickname (`quiz_players`) for the top-10 leaderboard; `/api/quiz/[code]/me` gives a player their own score and rank.
  - Not done yet: changing the right answer after people have answered doesn't re-score them; "Run it again" on a played question only lets people who haven't answered it join in.

- Open answers: the owner can hide one (`PATCH /api/admin/sessions/[code]/text`, `text_answers.hidden`, keyed by `text_answers.id` so the voter is never exposed). Hidden answers stay in exports marked "Hidden answer" and are never sent to the AI.
- Pick several has no "at least N" setting. Add one if presenters ask for it.
- Audience Q&A: each person can send up to 3 questions (`MAX_QA_PER_VOTER`). Voters' phones read the list from `/api/qa/[code]`, which the CDN caches for 3 seconds, so a phone shows its own question and vote straight away and the server count catches up (`qaVoterView` in `lib/answers.ts`).

How each type is built, so a new one follows the same pattern: kind check and settings columns in `db/schema.sql` (idempotent, at the end; the kind check is added once, in the last section, with every kind: add the new kind there, and recreate `live_sessions` if you add columns to `sessions`); kinds and answer schemas in `lib/validation.ts`; charts in `lib/charts.ts`; labels in `lib/kinds.ts`; save, results, exports, reset and edit counts in `lib/queries.ts`; maths in `lib/answers.ts`; the voter card in `components/vote/`; the results view in `components/results/`; tests in `test/question-types.test.ts`, `test/qa.test.ts`, `test/quiz.test.ts` and `test/answers-view.test.ts`. Write the tests first.

### Team activities (branch `feat/team-activities`, live)

Built test-first on 2026-11-14 (`test/activities.test.ts`, `test/activities-ui.test.ts`), checked in a browser at 390px and desktop (Paper and Midnight). **Live since 2026-11-14** (schema `db/2026-11-14-team-activities.sql` run first, then merged into `main`; production checked at 390px and desktop).

Why: team building is a pain point for teams, and most of it ends up offline, which leaves remote people out. LiveRate already gets everyone onto the same screen from their phones, so these activities work for people in the room and online alike. It's a differentiator: Mentimeter and Slido are built around polls, and retro tools don't do team building.

- **Boards** (`lib/boards.ts`, `focus_topics.board`: `tree` / `wall` / `sky`, Open answer only): open answers drawn as leaves on a tree, sticky notes on a cork wall or stars in a night sky (`components/results/BoardView.tsx`), instead of a list. **Each person can add up to `MAX_LEAVES` = 5 leaves** to a board (`text_answers.slot` 0–4, part of the primary key; plain open answers always use slot 0, so they stay one per person). The phone card (`components/vote/BoardCard.tsx`) lists "Your leaves (2 of 5)" with Change and Remove, then "Add another leaf"; each leaf saves on its own (`leafKey` = `<question>#<slot>`, `{ slot, text }` or `{ slot, remove: true }` to `/api/answer`). A board counts as done at 5 leaves or after "That's all from me", so one leaf doesn't end the activity.
- **The tree always fits the screen** (`sceneSize`): as wide as there's room for, never taller than the screen, and when it starts in the top 60% of the screen, short enough to be seen whole without scrolling (re-measured as things above it load). Leaves are as big as they can be without touching (`leafSize` in `lib/tree.ts`). Zoom with − / + / Fit (`ZOOMS` 1, 1.5, 2, 3) or pinch; drag to look around (`clampPan` keeps the picture covering its frame); words appear on leaves once they're big enough to read (font ≥ 9px after zoom). Checked at 360×640, 390×844, 768×1024, 1280×720, 1440×900 and 1920×1080. In the builder's preview it fits the pretend 1280×800 screen instead of the window (`data-screen`, `screenInFrame`). While a leaf, note or star is shown big, the keys belong to that box: Esc closes it and the arrows step through, without also going back to All questions or changing the question behind it. A results view of its own (chart `board`, named after the board by `chartsFor` in `lib/charts.ts`, the default for board questions; "Answer wall" and "One at a time" still work). The tree starts with bare branches in front of a soft pale crown and grows a leaf per answer; each answer keeps its spot as more arrive (`tree().leaf(i)` / `boardSpot`, by arrival order `arrivalOrder`, which uses `created`). Tap a piece to show it big (arrows step through; the owner can Hide it there). Long answers (up to 280 characters) fit: the leaf on the tree shows the first 3 lines with "…"; shown big, the words get smaller with length (`pieceTextSize`: big up to 40 characters, medium up to 120, small beyond), only short ones keep the pointed leaf shape, and the box never gets taller than the screen (it scrolls inside, the buttons stay in view). The phone's "Your leaves" list shows up to 4 lines of each, with Change and Remove under it. Phones draw leaves too small to read, so the "Read all N leaves" list starts open below 560px. Colours go through `tinted()`, so Mono turns them grey.
- **Names on leaves** (`text_answers.author`, up to `MAX_AUTHOR` = 30 characters): team building needs names, so every board leaf carries one. The phone card ("Write your leaf", leaf-shaped box) has a required "Your name" box and "Add my leaf" stays off until it's filled; the server refuses an unsigned board leaf (400 `name_required`); taking a leaf back needs no name. One name per person per presentation (`liverate:author:<code>` in localStorage), sent with every board answer. Plain open answers never store a name and stay anonymous; the phone header says "Answers on a board show your name. The rest are anonymous." when a question is a board, otherwise "Your answers are anonymous". The builder preview's sample leaves are all signed.
- **Profanity filter** (`lib/profanity.ts`, `test/profanity.test.ts`): a board leaf or its name with a bad word never reaches the board. `BAD_WORDS` is one list (English swearing, insults aimed at people such as idiot, stupid, moron, slurs, Hindi/Hinglish abuse incl. bewakoof) where each entry says how it matches: `word` alone, `word+` with usual endings (-s, -ed, -ing, -er, -y, -head, -hole…), `word*` starting a word, `*word*` anywhere inside one; `SAFE` holds ordinary words that would otherwise be caught (shiitake). `hasBadWords` first undoes disguises: case, accents, look-alike numbers and symbols (sh1t, a$$, @ss, b!tch), stretched letters (fuuuck), spaced or dotted letters (f u c k, s.h.i.t) and stars (f*ck, a**hole, at least half real letters). It leaves Scunthorpe, class, assessment, cocktail, Dickens, therapist, Arsenal, cocky, chutney and the like alone. The phone checks before sending and shows `BLOCKED_MESSAGE` ("That has a word we keep off the board…"), cleared as they retype; the server refuses anyway (400 `blocked_words`, which the vote queue drops as invalid). To add a word, add it to `BAD_WORDS` and a case to the test. Names show on the board, in the dialog, in the list, on the answer wall, and in exports (`answer — Name`).
- **Activity collection** (`lib/activities.ts`, 14 activities, each with an icon, length, how-to-run steps and a plan): Motivation tree, Goals for next year (sky), Kudos wall, Sailboat retro (wind / anchor / rocks / island), Weather check-in, Hidden superpowers, This or that, Our team in one word, Team agreements. `activityToBody` = `planToBody` + `showResults: true` (everyone watches the board fill up on their phone). Plans can carry `board` and `description`.
- **The tree drawing** (`lib/tree.ts`, `components/TreeArt.tsx`, `test/tree-art.test.ts`; redrawn 2026-11-14, **live since 2026-11-14**; production checked at 390px and desktop): `tree("wide")` (big screens) and `tree("tall")` (phones, and the home page) are two trees built from fixed seeds, measured in picture widths so the tree is never stretched. Limbs grow towards points spread through a round crown with a flatter bottom ("space colonisation": `grow`), so twigs reach every part of it; one-step side shoots are pruned, kinks are eased out, and thickness follows what each limb feeds. `TreeArt` draws sun, clouds, hills, a soft pale crown behind the branches (no small bud leaves: they looked like a second, smaller kind of leaf), the trunk with its root flare, and the limbs. **Leaves**: every leaf is the same shape (`BLADE`: the same above and below its middle vein, `LeafShape`) and the same size. Each has its stalk on a twig (`leaf(i)`: spots a little apart along the twigs, a few poses each) and grows out of it like a real one: turned 45° or 60° off the twig, pointing the way the twig grows (20°–75° off it, never back towards the trunk), tipped at most 32° up, level wherever the twig allows and never more than 20° down. **No overlap**: the order of the spots is worked out so that the first N leaves are as far from touching as possible (`touchSize`: each blade is five circles, the answer is exact), and `leafSize(n)` is the biggest size at which none of the first N touch or leave the picture, between 3.4% and 12% of the width (6%–24% on the tall tree). Earlier leaves never move; all of them shrink together as more arrive. ### Abuse limits (security sweep 2026-11-09, brought into `feat/ux-round` 2026-11-21)

Ships with `db/2026-11-21-follow-moderation.sql` (its four tables: `rate_hits`, `session_voters`, `hidden_words`, `quiz_hidden_players`), which must run on production **before** the code. Optional: set `RATE_LIMIT_SECRET` in Vercel.

Built (`lib/limits.ts`, `lib/voter-gate.ts`, `test/abuse-limits.test.ts`):

- **Per-address request limits**, counted in the database (`rate_hits`; the bucket holds a salted hash of the address, salt `RATE_LIMIT_SECRET` or `CRON_SECRET`; the daily purge deletes counters older than a day). The address comes from `x-real-ip` / `x-forwarded-for` (set by Vercel); with none, there's no limit outside production. Limits (`LIMITS`), sized so a 100-person workshop on one Wi-Fi fits: password checks (log in, sign up, reset, save guest) 300 per 10 min; sign-ups 100 an hour; new guests 100 an hour; guest-code sign-ins 100 per 10 min; answers 6,000 a minute (a 100-person room on one Wi-Fi scoring 15 things in the same minute is 1,500); new voter ids 1,000 an hour. Over a limit = 429 `too_many_requests` with `Retry-After`.
- **Voters per presentation**: at most `MAX_VOTERS_PER_PRESENTATION` = 1,000 (`session_voters`); a new voter after that gets 403 `full`. "Clear all answers" empties it. Every answer route (`/api/vote`, `/api/answer`, `/api/qa/[code]`, `/api/quiz/[code]`) goes through `admitVoter` first.
- **No cache-busting**: the CDN-cached endpoints (status, Q&A list, public results) refuse any query string (400) before touching the database.
- **Password hashing only after the limit**; a reset link that can't work is refused before hashing.
- **Lockouts per address**: 10 wrong passwords lock that address out of that email (`email|address` in `login_failures`); 100 from everywhere lock the email (`total:email`). Guest codes: per-address limit, plus a site-wide brake at 2,000 wrong codes per 10 min. New guests site-wide: 1,000 an hour.
- **Caps**: a saved account keeps up to `ACCOUNT_PRESENTATION_LIMIT` = 100 presentations (403 `account_limit`); AI has a site-wide daily cap `AI_SITE_DAILY_LIMIT` (default 500) on top of each account's.
- **Owner hides on the big screen**: word-cloud words (`PATCH /api/admin/sessions/[code]/words`, `hidden_words`; exports mark them "Hidden word", Markdown leaves them out, phones never get them) and quiz players (`POST …/quiz` `hide_player` / `show_player`, by the leaderboard row's `id` = md5 of presentation + voter, `quiz_hidden_players`; `hiddenPlayers` in the owner's results).
